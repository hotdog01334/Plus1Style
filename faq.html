FAQ

How did +1 style get its name?:

Siddarth came up with it due to it being a reference to when you level up and get a +1 to a stat in video-games, but since the brand is about fashion as well, style was the perfect stat to describe it

Why are there only 9 shirts?:

There are only 9 shirts due to us being a new business and trying to work with the funds we have

Why both merch and cosplay?

We want to attract non-gamers, and non-cosplayers that are still gamers as well, which is why we also chose to do merch

Are you officially collabed with the brands of the merch you have?

IPBA answer: Yes, we have collaborated with the owners to make merch out of their brands/ips. Real-life answer: No

Did you actually make the outfits shown in the cosplay tab?

IPBA answer: Yes, we have made these outfits out of quality material and made sure we put our time and effort into them. Real-life answer: No, go support the original creators because they put a lot of hard work into making those costumes
